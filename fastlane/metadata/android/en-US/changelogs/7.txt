Three defects that only showed up in real use on a phone.

Invitations sent from the app did not open. The link was built from the
internal address the app serves itself from, which exists inside that one app
on that one phone and nowhere else on earth: whoever received it got "site
cannot be reached". Every invitation, QR code and shared address now points at
the public site.

Connections between devices on different networks never completed. The side
creating the invitation shut down the mechanism carrying the other side's
network addresses, because the connection "wasn't working yet" — removing the
very thing that would have made it work. It only showed when the two were not
on the same network: between a phone on mobile data and a laptop on wi-fi,
always.

The header was still sliding under the clock. The previous fix shrank the
window from the inside, where the page could not feel it.

The launcher now reads DV Logos, so the whole name fits.
