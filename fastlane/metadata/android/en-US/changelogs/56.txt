Callers hear a ringback tone, and read "Ringing…" when it is true.

Before, calling an address only showed a spinner: no way to tell whether anything was happening. Now, as soon as the call goes out, you hear the "tu… tu… tu…" of a normal phone (generated by the app, no file, no server), and it stops the instant the other side answers or refuses. The relay also tells the caller whether the other phone actually received the wake: if so the title becomes "Ringing…", otherwise it stays "Calling…". The address card in settings now says "you can switch it off here whenever you want" instead of "off by default". Calls, ringer and protocol are the same as v55.
