No longer a wrapper around a website.

The entire application is now inside the package and is served from the app's
own storage, so what runs on the phone is what was built from this source — and
it keeps working when the website cannot be reached at all, which for people
under network censorship is the difference between having the tool and not.

The device's identity now lives in the app's own storage rather than the
browser's, where it could be evicted under storage pressure: losing it meant
losing your address and every contact's way of reaching you, with no account to
recover.
