A phone no longer goes deaf after one call that did not work.

Reported by two phones that stopped being able to reach each other by any
route — neither the permanent address nor a six-digit code — and came back
only when the app was force-closed.

The app keeps the last connection attempt around to know whether it is already
busy with someone. There was already an expiry for abandoned attempts, but it
covered a single state. An attempt that got halfway and stalled never expired,
and halfway is exactly where a call stops when two devices cannot reach each
other.

From then on the phone believed it was busy for good: it refused every incoming
call and every new attempt, with nothing on screen to say so and no way out but
killing the app.

The expiry now applies to any attempt that never managed to connect at all. A
real conversation goes on counting as busy for as long as it lasts, an hour
included.
