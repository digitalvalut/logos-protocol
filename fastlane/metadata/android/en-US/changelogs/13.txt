Throwaway addresses now say what they do not do, as well as what they do.

What they said was true: delete the address and that person can no longer reach
you, and they never had your permanent one. But it let people believe something
more, and that something is not true.

A throwaway address hides which address you own. It does not make you a
different person: the three-word security code comes from the device's
certificate, which stays the same. Anyone who has already read those three words
with you finds them unchanged, and knows it is you.

Reported by someone who connected through a throwaway code and found the earlier
conversation waiting there, and quite reasonably did not understand why. It was
not a defect — it was something the app had never said.
