The phone rings right away, even with the app closed.

Until now, with the app closed, Logos looked at its own mailbox every 5, 45 or 90 seconds (depending on how recently you had used it): a caller could wait up to a minute and a half before you rang. The phone now keeps an open wire to the relay — a wire that sleeps and costs nothing — and the relay pulls it the instant an envelope arrives: you ring within a couple of seconds, every time. No Google, no third-party app, and the relay learns nothing new (it already saw that "someone looks at mailbox X"; it now sees it continuously instead of in samples).

If the wire drops, or the relay cannot hold it, the phone goes back to looking at the mailbox as before: nothing is lost, only the wait changes. Only the Android app and the relay change; the page is the same as v47.
