Version 5 refused to install on some phones, saying only "App not installed".

It carried seven permissions and four components more than the version before
it — foreground service, notifications, wake lock, start at boot — all for
ringing while the app is closed, which was switched off in that same version.
Asking for permissions on behalf of a feature that does nothing is wrong on its
own terms. It no longer asks, and what it declares is now exactly what version
4 declared.

Both fixes from version 5 are still here: the logo, and the header that no
longer slides underneath the clock.
