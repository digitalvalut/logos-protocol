When a call drops, the phone becomes reachable again.

A fix to a fix. Yesterday's release addressed a connection attempt that stalled
halfway and left the phone believing it was busy for good. That fix, however,
exempted from expiry any connection that had managed to connect at least once —
and a dropped call is neither closed nor failed, so it sat in that exemption
forever.

The result was the same silence as before, reached by another route: one dropped
call and nobody could reach you again.

Time is now counted from the last moment the connection actually worked rather
than from when it was created. A call that dropped a moment ago still blocks,
because in a lift or a tunnel it comes back on its own. One that dropped and
never returned stops blocking after forty-five seconds.
