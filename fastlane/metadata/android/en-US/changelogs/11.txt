No change to the application: it is identical to version 10.

Only the way the package is signed is different. F-Droid rebuilds the app from
the public source and compares it against the one published here: the two must
come out bit for bit identical, and that is how anyone can check that the
package contains nothing the source does not.

The comparison was failing on an internal alignment difference introduced by
the signing tool. The signature now preserves the original layout, and the
rebuild comes out identical.
