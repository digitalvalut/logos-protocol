Two things that were plainly wrong on a phone.

The logo did not appear. The single-file build looked for it beside itself, as
a separate file — which works on the website, where that file sits next to the
page, and cannot work inside the package, where nothing sits beside anything.
The logo is now part of the file it belongs to.

The header was sliding underneath the clock and the battery, cut in half. An app
targeting a recent Android is laid out edge to edge whether it asks to be or
not, and it is the app's job to keep clear of the system bars. It now does, on
every version rather than only the ones that force the issue.
