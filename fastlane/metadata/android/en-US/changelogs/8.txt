The keyboard no longer covers what you are typing.

This was the unpaid price of the previous fix: an app drawn edge to edge is no
longer resized by Android for anything, the keyboard included. The keyboard is
now part of the calculation.

Call permissions are asked for one at a time. The app used to demand the
microphone and the camera together, so anyone who had turned the camera down
could not place a voice call either — and was told the microphone was busy.

And a refused microphone now says so. The refusal used to reach the page as
"the microphone is in use by another app", which sent people looking for the
problem in the wrong place entirely.
