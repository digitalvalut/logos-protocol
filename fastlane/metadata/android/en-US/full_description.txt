Private chat, voice and video calls and file sharing, directly between two
devices: no account, no phone number, no server that ever sees a message.

Two devices open a private WebRTC connection and talk directly, end-to-end
encrypted. A small relay only helps them find each other and, on very strict
networks, forwards traffic that is already encrypted and that it cannot read.
No database anywhere holds conversations. The relay is a Cloudflare Worker run
by the project (digitalvalut-turn.burbeng78.workers.dev); its source is in the
same repository (turn-worker/).

<b>How you reach someone</b>

• A permanent address (DV-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) that checks itself: it is the hash of
  a key made on your device, so a swapped key is rejected
• Up to eight throwaway addresses, each one revocable on its own
• An invite link or a QR code, optionally locked with a passphrase you say out
  loud
• Their phone rings even with the app closed, with no Google and no Firebase
  (off until switched on)
• A sealed letter for someone who cannot answer, kept up to 7 days

<b>Knowing who you are talking to</b>

The first time you connect, both sides see the same three-word security code,
taken from the real cryptographic handshake: if the words match, nobody is in
the middle. Contacts and blocking trust only the identity the encrypted
connection proves, never a name someone declares.

<b>What it does</b>

• Text, emoji, voice messages, photos, video and files up to 512 MB, at
  original quality
• Photos lose their GPS location and device data on the phone before sending,
  without being re-encoded
• Audio and video calls, with screen sharing
• Share into Logos straight from any other app
• Self-destructing messages and optional automatic cleanup
• 13 languages, right-to-left for Arabic and Urdu, instructions read aloud and
  three text sizes

<b>Extra protection, off until you want it</b>

• Protected screen: no screenshots and a black preview among open apps, against
  spyware and prying eyes
• Hide where you are: everything goes through the encrypted bridge, and the
  other person never sees your IP address
• A list of blocked people

<b>Built to be checked, not trusted</b>

• Its connection protocol is written out and checked with formal tools
  (Tamarin, ProVerif, TLA+), which found real attacks that were then closed
• Reproducible: F-Droid rebuilds it from source and gets the same package
• No third-party library, script or CDN; all cryptography is the system's own
  Web Crypto
• The whole app is inside the package, not a website in a frame: it keeps
  working when the website cannot be reached

<b>Honest limits</b>

A live conversation needs both people connected, but not with the app open:
their phone rings even when it is closed, and if it is off, a sealed letter
waits for them. Without "Hide where you are", your IP address is visible to the
person you connect to, as in any direct connection.
